# Suno features beyond the prompt boxes

Reach for these when the request is about reusing a sound or reworking an existing track, not writing a fresh prompt.

Source: https://help.suno.com/en/categories/550017

## Reusing a sound

**Personas** — save the essence of a song (its vocals, style) and recall it for new songs. Create: song's ⋯ menu → Create → Make Persona; name it, add avatar and description, set public or private (defaults to public). Use: Library → Personas tab, or pick one in Custom mode from the area above the Lyrics field — its style details auto-fill Style of Music.

This is the right answer whenever someone wants a consistent artist voice across several tracks. It beats trying to re-describe the same vocal in prose every time.

**Custom Models** (v5.5, Pro/Premier) — build a model from your own catalogue. Upload as few as six songs you own the rights to; up to three models; ready in about 2–5 minutes.

**Voices** (v5.5, Pro/Premier) — record your own vocal signature and have Suno's composition performed in it.

**My Taste** (all tiers, including free) — learns from listening and generation habits. It steers recommendations and how defaults lean; it does not change a song you explicitly prompt for.

**Inspire** — generate an original track matching the stylistic character of a selected playlist.

## Reworking an existing track

**Extend** — lengthen a song or give it a new ending. Models generate up to 8 minutes before extending.

**Crop** (Pro/Premier) — trim the beginning or end.

**Replace Section** (Pro/Premier) — regenerate one part of a song in place. The targeted fix when a single verse is wrong and everything else is right.

**Cover** — keep the composition, change the sound.

**Remaster** — clean up older songs.

**ReMi** — Suno's alternate lyrics model, for a different lyric-writing character.

**Add Vocals** — turn an instrumental into a full vocal track.

**Song Editor** — post-generation editing, including reordering sections.

**Advanced Stem Separation** — split a song into stems with customisable options.

## Bringing audio in

**Upload Audio** — turn any sound into music: transform a rough demo, or generate an instrumental around what you recorded. Enables the **Audio Influence** slider; raise it when the uploaded melody, rhythm, phrasing, or vocal identity has to carry through.

**Suno Sounds** — generate individual sound effects, instrument samples, and ambient noises rather than whole songs.

## Housekeeping

**Workspaces** — organise projects.

Songs default to private; the home page only shows what you've made public.

Suno moderates generations. Prompts naming a real artist get rejected — describe the sound instead. Original lyrics a user supplies remain theirs.
