# Creative

Research this skill came from — read it first: `~/search/creativity-first-principles.md`

My first idea is the most probable one, and the audience predicts it too. Aim for surprising, then inevitable.

1. Frame: write the real goal and who will meet the result. Write 3 other framings. Pick the richest one.
2. Ban: write the 3 ideas that come first. Ban them and anything close to them.
3. Diverge: write 15 or more ideas, each from a different lever, each with a rough probability. Keep the tails (under 10%).
   - join two far domains by a real shared structure
   - break or flip one rule of the genre
   - add one hard limit, or take away a core part
   - change the audience or the scale
4. Stuck: jump to a far domain. Flowing: stay close and go deeper.
5. Pick with a stranger's eye: score new × works × obvious-once-seen. Distrust "safe" and distrust my favourite. Keep 3 that differ.
6. Build the winner: a conventional core plus one strong twist. One idea, repeated with variation. Cut the rest.
7. Check: can a stranger get it in one beat? If not, simplify the core, not the twist.
   Then critique brutally: timeline, motive, continuity, honesty, cost. Fix and repeat until no flaw is left.
8. Show the winner, plus one wild alternate in one line.
